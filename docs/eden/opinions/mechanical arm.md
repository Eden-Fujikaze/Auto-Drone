## Goal
Have a manually/automatically operating industry line hand that has the capability of moving a simple shape to its matching destination (predefined).
## Steps
1. No. of servos to define range
2. A way to make the arm enclose and be able to grip stuff
3. Rotation and solid base to have it not tip over
4. Camera for recognition of object to move and its desired spot
5. Inverse Kinematic to auto control - rotation, servos and grip when operating
6. Operable UI and camera feed with confidence
## Troubles
Modeling and soldering it correctly. Learning all about IK and wiring it up correctly. Meticoulous testing and if not done algorithmically, RL will take time.
